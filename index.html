About VS Design & Build

VS Design & Build is a family-run main contractor with decades of industry experience. We deliver exceptional quality across 8 key sectors:

Commercial

Complete transformation and modernization of interior office spaces, structural refurbishments, and high-end fit-outs.

Education

State-of-the-art updates for universities and schools, creating collaborative learning environments and cutting-edge facilities.

Retail

High-street fit-outs and specialized commercial spaces designed to withstand heavy footfall while maintaining luxury design.

Residential

Premium refurbishment, structural adjustments, and high-quality internal and external decorations for residential properties.

Hospitality & Leisure

High-end restaurant, hotel, and boutique bar transformations, delivering stunning visitor experiences under tight deadlines.

Healthcare

Specialized contractor services for medical buildings and clinics, ensuring safe operations and pristine finishes.

Industrial

Robust design, layout reconstruction, and maintenance solutions for industrial premises and warehouses.

Heritage

Expert restoration, leadwork, masonry repairs, and traditional redecorations for Grade I and II listed monuments.
